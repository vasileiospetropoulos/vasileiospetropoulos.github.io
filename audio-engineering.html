Film Sound Design & Foley — Cosmos

The original footage already contained the dialogue between the sheep and the human character. I created the rest of the soundscape, adding Foley, sound effects and ambience to match the on-screen action. Some sounds were recorded by me, while others came from sound libraries such as Soundsnap. I edited, synchronised and mixed everything in Avid Pro Tools.
