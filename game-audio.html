Fantasy Combat Sound Design

AUDIO STORYTELLING

Medieval Fantasy — Audio Storytelling

A collaborative audio-only fantasy story created by Vasileios Petropoulos and a fellow sound designer. The listener travels through a harbor, a tavern fight, a rain-soaked castle and a dragon battle. With no visuals to follow, the sound invites each listener to imagine the places, characters and events in their own way.

Listen on SoundCloud ↗

UNITY · GAME AUDIO

Survival Template — Unity Sound Design

A team project using the Survival Template in Unity 2022.3.49f1. My contribution included footsteps, UI feedback, crafting and building Foley, environmental ambience, creature sounds and player feedback audio.
