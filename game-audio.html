Selected Work · Interactive & Film Audio

Game Audio & Sound Design.

Sound effects, Foley, ambience, audio storytelling and interactive sound design. These projects showcase different approaches to creating sound for games, film and fantasy environments.

SOUND DESIGN · FANTASY

Fantasy Combat Sound Design

A collection of five short magic-combat effects, including an area-of-effect ground blast, arcane energy, a light beam, a sword barrage and a cryo freeze spell.

FILM · FOLEY

Film Sound Design & Foley — Cosmos

A film sound project created in Pro Tools, adding Foley and ambience to picture alongside the existing dialogue. The soundscape combines recorded material and sound-library assets.

AUDIO STORYTELLING

Medieval Fantasy — Audio Storytelling

An audio-only fantasy narrative built around a harbor quest, a tavern fight, a rainy castle, a dragon battle and the resolution, using layered effects and environments.

UNITY · GAME AUDIO

Survival Template — Unity Sound Design

A team project using the Survival Template in Unity 2022.3.49f1. My contribution included footsteps, UI feedback, crafting and building Foley, environmental ambience, creature sounds and player feedback audio.

Audio and video previews will be added to the project cards as they are prepared for online viewing.
