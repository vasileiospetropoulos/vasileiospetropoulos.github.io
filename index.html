About Me

Creative work meets technology.

I am an audio engineer and music producer from Athens, Greece. I have completed my studies for a Bachelor of Arts in Audio at SAE Athens, specialising in Game Audio, and I am currently studying Informatics at the University of Piraeus.

My interests include recording, editing, mixing, mastering, music production, beatmaking, sound design and audio storytelling. I also have knowledge of Python, Java and C#, and enjoy combining creative thinking with technical problem-solving.
